## Seliem Attia

I work on search visibility — mostly the part that is now decided inside AI
answer engines rather than on a results page. Based in the German/Swiss border
region.

Most of that work is measurement, and the interesting question is rarely
"are we visible". It is whether the number you just measured would come out the
same way twice.

**[aeo-audit](https://github.com/Jerry-Joe-Pete/aeo-audit)** — measures two
things separately that most tools blend into one: whether a brand is *named* in
an AI answer, and whether its domain is *cited* as a source. Every query runs
five times, so the report carries a stability count instead of a disclaimer.

In the example audit, the audited brand was named in 56 of 58 answers and cited
in 18 — and 8 of 12 queries returned a different result across five identical
runs.
